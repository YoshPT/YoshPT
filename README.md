## 🚀 Projects

### 1. What factors are linked to event no-shows?

> **💡 Insight:** Lead time and job role were the main factors associated with no-shows (9,000+ registrations, logistic regression, AUC 0.70).

Economics senior project with Eventthai and Chiang Mai University. Fitted a binary logistic regression in R on anonymized event-registration data and interpreted odds ratios with 95% CIs, p-values, and GVIF checks for multicollinearity. An AUC of 0.70 means moderate discrimination, so the goal is to explain the drivers of no-shows rather than build a precise predictor. Recommendations: overbooking quotas and targeted CRM reminders.

🛠️ `R` `RStudio` · 🔒 Data proprietary (not published) · [**View project →**](https://github.com/YoshPT/event-noshow-prediction)

<br>

### 2. How unequal is Thai household income? A group-level view

> **💡 Insight:** Managers/professionals earn ~3.1× agricultural laborers. Chanthaburi tops the province gap ranking, but mainly because of one extreme group (tenant farmers).

Group-level analysis of NSO 2023 household income data, from data cleaning to province comparisons and income-source structure, using the Gini coefficient and coefficient of variation (CV). Means and Gini are unweighted across occupation groups. Built for Super AI Engineer Season 6 (mini-hackathon, Mar 2026).

🛠️ `Python` `Pandas` `Matplotlib` `Seaborn` · [**View project →**](https://github.com/YoshPT/Thailand-Income-Inequality-EDA)

<br>

### 3. Who are our most valuable customers?

> **💡 Insight:** Champions and Loyal customers, just 15% of the base, generate ~70% of revenue.

RFM segmentation in SQL on a public Kaggle sales dataset: 812 customers (2,000 transactions) classified into 6 segments for targeted marketing, with cross-sell opportunities and a win-back list of high-value "At Risk" accounts. Results are visualized in an interactive Tableau dashboard.

🛠️ `SQL` `MySQL` `CTEs` `Window Functions` `Tableau` · [**View project →**](https://github.com/YoshPT/SQL-RFM-Customer-Segmentation) · [**Dashboard →**](https://public.tableau.com/views/RFMCustomerSegmentation_17952664681280/RFMCustomerSegmentation)
